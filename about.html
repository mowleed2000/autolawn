Our Heritage

Honest Workmanship, No Compromises

Incorporated in April 2015, Woodlawn Auto Centre has grown from a local workshop into one of Hounslow's most trusted independent vehicle repair destinations. Under the leadership of Mr. Shah and senior technicians like Arash, our philosophy is simple: diagnose accurately, quote fairly, and perform every repair to the highest safety and engineering standard.

Unlike national franchises that push unnecessary add-ons, our team takes the time to explain what your vehicle genuinely needs. We inspect parts with you, provide transparent itemised quotes, and treat your vehicle with the care it deserves.
