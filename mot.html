Official MOT Inspection You Can Trust

As an officially licensed Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) MOT Test Station (VTS Site #18611), Woodlawn Auto Centre has been inspecting cars, taxis, 3-wheelers, and light commercial vehicles on Hanworth Road since 2015.

Our test lane uses calibrated roller brake testers, headlamp alignment rigs, and exhaust emission analysers to test your vehicle accurately and objectively. If your car passes, we immediately update the government DVSA database and issue your certificate.

✓ Class 4: Passenger cars up to 8 seats, motor caravans, private dual-purpose vehicles, taxis, and ambulances.
✓ Class 3: 3-wheeled vehicles over 450kg unladen weight.
✓ Free Retest: If minor repairs are needed, return within 10 working days for a free retest.
✓ On-Site Repairs: Bulbs, wipers, tyres, or brake adjustments can be resolved while you wait.
MOT Inspection Highlights

What We Check

  • Lighting & Signalling: Headlamps, indicators, brake lights, fog lamps & aim
  • Braking System: Efficiency, balance, pads, discs, pipes, and handbrake
  • Steering & Suspension: Ball joints, shock absorbers, springs & bearings
  • Tyres & Wheels: Tread depth (1.6mm legal limit), condition, size & pressure
  • Seatbelts & Restraints: Condition, locking mechanisms, airbags & mounts
  • Body & Chassis: Structural integrity, corrosion checks & sharp edges
  • Exhaust & Emissions: Gas emissions testing, catalytic converter & noise levels
  • Driver's View: Wipers, washers, windscreen cracks, and mirrors
