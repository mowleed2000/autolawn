Everything you need to know about testing, servicing, warranties, and repairs at Woodlawn Auto Centre on Hanworth Road.

We are located at 644 Hanworth Road, Hounslow, Greater London, TW4 5NP. Our workshop features customer parking, dedicated MOT testing bays, and a full body repair facility.

Our workshop is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm (09:00–18:00). We are closed on Sundays.

Our official DVSA VTS site number is 18611. We are licensed by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency to test Class 4 vehicles (standard passenger cars, taxis, and light commercial vehicles up to 3,000kg) and Class 3 vehicles (3-wheeled motor vehicles).

If your vehicle fails, we issue a clear itemised explanation of the dangerous or major defects. If you have the required repairs completed by us (or return the vehicle within 10 working days), your MOT retest is completely free of charge.
